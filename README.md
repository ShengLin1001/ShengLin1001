### Hi there 👋

I am a PhD student in materials engineering at Zhejiang University. My research focuses on phase transformations, deformation control, and dislocation theory in metallic materials.

* 🔭 I received my master's degree through Zhejiang University and Moscow Aviation University in July 2024.

* 🌱 I am currently working on materials science, computational materials modeling, and Python-based scientific programming.

* ⚡ I developed the `mymetal` package for modeling and post-processing thin-film materials and heterostructures. This package provides a mechanical framework for addressing the adaptation problem in complex heterojunctions, going beyond conventional lattice-mismatch descriptions. I am experienced in first-principles calculations of key material properties, including structural optimization, formation energy, deformation energy, surface energy, COHP/ICOHP, DOS, and band structures.

* 🔬 My current research focuses on phase transformations and deformation control in precious metals, with particular interest in dislocation-mediated mechanisms.

* 💬 Feel free to ask me anything. I am always happy to discuss materials science, computational modeling, and scientific programming.
